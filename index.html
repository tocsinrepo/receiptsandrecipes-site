Recipe teaser: Dumpling and Broccoli Rice Bowl

Teaser recipe. Not yet kitchen-tested.

From the grocery haul: frozen pork dumplings, broccoli florets, medium grain rice, soy sauce, hoisin. Serves 2.

  1. Cook 1 cup rice.
  2. Cook 10 dumplings as the bag says; steam broccoli 4 minutes.
  3. Stir 1 tbsp hoisin + 1 tbsp soy + 1 tbsp water.
  4. Serve dumplings and broccoli over rice with the sauce.
